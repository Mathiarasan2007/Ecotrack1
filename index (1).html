THE PROBLEM

The problem is bigger than a single full bin.

Every day, city streets face a breakdown in the sanitation chain. Waste is tossed without segregation, communal bins overflow unnoticed for days, and local sanitation departments struggle with manual reporting paths that lead nowhere.

🗑️ Mixed Waste

Dry recyclables contaminated with organic wet waste reduce recycling facility yield to under 20% and trigger toxic landfill buildup.

📍 Missed Collection

Static truck schedules leave peripheral sectors unattended while quiet neighborhoods receive repetitive unnecessary passes.

⏱️ Slow Response

Citizens submit complaints through buried phone helplines, leading to 7-day latency intervals before a cleanup squad is dispatched.

👁️ No Visibility

Lack of photographic proof and GPS tracking means municipalities have zero verifiable feedback on resolution.

WASTE MANAGEMENT

Proper segregation is the foundation of an efficient waste system.

Wet / Organic

Wet / Organic Waste

Biodegradable materials that break down naturally into compost. Keep liquid contents drained before disposal.

  • Food scraps & vegetable peels
  • Yard waste & grass clippings
  • Soiled paper & cardboard
  • Coconut shell & jute twine
Dry / Recyclable

Dry / Recyclable Waste

Clean, non-biodegradable items that can be remanufactured. Must be rinsed clean of grease and food oils.

  • Paper, cardboard & magazines
  • Plastic bottles & containers
  • Glass & aluminum cans
  • Metal & tin foil
Special / Hazardous

Special & Hazardous

Items posing chemical, biological, or electronic contamination. Never mix into standard residential dumpsters.

  • Medical waste & needles
  • Chemical & pesticide containers
  • E-waste & batteries
  • Paint & oil cans
